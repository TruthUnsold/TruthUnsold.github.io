# Define

- **Hebrew:** av (אָב) — father. Across the Hebrew Bible it covers several overlapping roles:
    - **Biological father:** "a man will leave his father and his mother" (Genesis 2:24).
    - **Ancestor or forefather:** the "father" can be several generations back (Genesis 17:4-5; 2 Kings 14:3; Isaiah 51:2).
    - **Head of the household and clan:** authority over the "father's house," beit av (בֵּית־אָב), and the one who passes on instruction and covenant (Genesis 18:19; Exodus 12:3; Deuteronomy 6:6-7; Proverbs 1:8).
    - **Founder or originator of a trade or people:** Jabal "the father of those who dwell in tents," Jubal "the father of all who handle the harp and pipe" (Genesis 4:20-21).
    - **Protector, provider, and counselor:** Joseph "a father to Pharaoh" (Genesis 45:8); "a father to the needy" (Job 29:16); Eliakim "a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem" (Isaiah 22:21).
    - **Title of honor for a priest or prophet:** "be to me a father and a priest" (Judges 17:10); "My father, my father" said of Elijah (2 Kings 2:12).
    - **A title for God:** the one who made, bought, and established his people (Deuteronomy 32:6); the one who has compassion (Psalm 103:13); the one who corrects in love (Proverbs 3:12; Isaiah 63:16; Malachi 2:10).
- **Greek (LXX):** pater (πατήρ) — the LXX renders av with pater throughout (Genesis 4:20; Genesis 45:8; Deuteronomy 32:6). The NT keeps the same range:
    - **Biological father:** Matthew 2:22.
    - **Ancestor:** "Abraham as our father" (Matthew 3:9; Luke 1:73; John 8:39).
    - **Elder or leader addressed with respect:** "Brothers and fathers" (Acts 7:2; Acts 22:1).
    - **Spiritual father:** "I became your father through the Good News" (1 Corinthians 4:15).
    - **Father by character or works:** "You are of your father the devil" (John 8:41-44).
    - **God as Father:** Matthew 6:9; Romans 8:15; Hebrews 12:9; James 1:17.

# Related Words

- avot (אָבוֹת) — fathers, forefathers (Exodus 3:15; Deuteronomy 1:8)
- beit av (בֵּית־אָב) — father's house, extended family unit (Exodus 12:3; Numbers 1:2)
- mishpachah (מִשְׁפָּחָה) — clan, family (Numbers 1:2)

- ben (בֵּן) — son (Proverbs 3:12)
- avinu (אָבִינוּ) — our Father (Isaiah 63:16)
- Avraham/[[Gate of Wisdom/Beings/Human Beings/Abraham\|Abraham]] (אַבְרָהָם) — father of a multitude (Genesis 17:5)

- patria (πατριά) — family, clan descended from a father (Luke 2:4; Ephesians 3:14-15)
- patriarches (πατριάρχης) — patriarch, ruling father (Acts 7:8-9; Hebrews 7:4)

# Additional Information

- Throughout Scripture, "father" is defined by relationship and function more than by birth: the one who gives life, leads, provides, protects, teaches, and passes on an inheritance (Genesis 18:19; Genesis 48:15-16; Numbers 27:8-11; Proverbs 13:22).
- The father carries the household's covenant responsibility, teaching the commands to the next generation (Deuteronomy 4:9; Deuteronomy 6:6-7; Psalm 78:3-6).
- Honoring father and mother is a command (Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16) [[Gate of Wisdom/Right Standing/Commands (Mitzvot)\|Commands (Mitzvot)]]. Dishonoring them carries a serious penalty (Exodus 21:15, 17; Proverbs 30:17).
- Fatherhood tied to God is often about [[Zunpublished/Covenant\|Covenant]] and compassion toward his people (Exodus 4:22; Deuteronomy 14:1; Psalm 68:5; Hosea 11:1).
- Yeshua/Jesus's teaching shifts weight to the Father in heaven as the one true Father (Matthew 23:9; Matthew 5:16; John 14:6-9).
