Word: Prophet (navi) — 2 Kings 14:25

# Define

- Hebrew: navi (נָבִיא) — "prophet, spokesman"; from a root likely related to "to call, announce, proclaim" — in Hebrew mindset, a navi is fundamentally one who speaks on behalf of another, specifically one who receives and delivers YHWH's own word rather than his own opinion or insight. The office carries the weight of being a mouthpiece, not an independent source
- "According to the word of Yahweh, the God of Israel, which he spoke by his servant Jonah the son of Amittai, the prophet (navi), who was of Gath Hepher" (2 Kings 14:25, WEB)
- Greek (LXX): prophetes (προφήτης) — "one who speaks forth/before"; the LXX renders navi consistently with this term, later carried directly into NT usage

# Related Words

- Naba (נָבָא) — the verb form, "to prophesy," describing the act of speaking as a navi
- Ro'eh (רֹאֶה) — "seer," an older or alternate term for the same office, explicitly noted as an earlier name for the role: "he who is now called a prophet (navi) was before called a seer (ro'eh)" (1 Samuel 9:9, WEB)
- Eved (עֶבֶד) — "servant," used of Jonah in the same verse ("his servant Jonah," 2 Kings 14:25) — pairing the prophetic office with the language of servanthood, a common biblical pairing for those who speak or act on YHWH's behalf
- Ish elohim (אִישׁ אֱלֹהִים) — "man of God," another closely related title applied to various prophetic figures (e.g., Elijah, 1 Kings 17:18)

# Additional Information

- 2 Kings 14:25 is the only place in Scripture that directly names Jonah "the prophet" (navi) — the book of Jonah itself never applies this title to him, referring to him only by name and patronymic ("Jonah the son of Amittai," Jonah 1:1)
- The prophetic office in the Tanakh does not require moral perfection to be legitimately held or exercised — as established earlier, Moses, Elijah, David (in a related but distinct anointed-office sense), and Jonah himself all continue to function in their calling despite significant recorded failures
- A navi's core task, as modeled throughout the Tanakh, is to speak exactly what YHWH gives, not to add, subtract, or interpret according to personal preference — this is the standard Jonah is explicitly shown meeting even while resisting internally: he delivers the message "according to the word of Yahweh" (2 Kings 14:25) both in his earlier restoration prophecy and in his proclamation to Nineveh (Jonah 3:4)

# Connections

- 2 Kings 14:25 — the sole direct designation of Jonah as navi
- 1 Samuel 9:9 — the seer/prophet terminology shift
- Deuteronomy 18:18-22 — Torah's own definition of a true prophet, including the test that a prophet's word must come to pass
- Jonah 3:4 — Jonah's proclamation, fulfilling the navi's core function of delivering YHWH's exact message
