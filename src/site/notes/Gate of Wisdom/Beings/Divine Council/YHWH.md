# Define
- Hebrew: יהוה (yhwh) - commonly rendered as "LORD"
	- The Divine Name of the [[Gate of Wisdom/Beings/Divine Council/God\|God]] of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. 

- Greek: kyrios (κύριος) – used for yhwh (יהוה) in Septuagint and New Testament quotations


# Related words

- [[Gate of Wisdom/Beings/Divine Council/Yah\|Yah]]

# Additional Info

In most English Bibles, "LORD" in small capitals stands for the divine name YHWH (often rendered Yahweh or Jehovah).

**YHWH/God states his name directly**

- **Exodus 3:14-15:** At the burning bush, God says "I AM THAT I AM," tells Moses to say "I AM hath sent me," then adds that "the LORD God of your fathers... this is my name for ever."
- **Exodus 6:2-3:** "I am the LORD: and I appeared unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, by the name of God Almighty, but by my name JEHOVAH was I not known to them."
- **Isaiah 42:8:** "I am the LORD: that is my name: and my glory will I not give to another."
- **Jeremiah 16:21:** "...and they shall know that my name is The LORD."
- **Exodus 34:5-7:** The LORD descends and proclaims "the name of the LORD" to Moses.

**Declared about God, with his name stated**

- **Exodus 15:3:** "The LORD is a man of war: the LORD is his name."
- **Psalm 83:18:** "That men may know that thou, whose name alone is JEHOVAH, art the most high over all the earth."
- **Psalm 68:4:** "...extol him that rideth upon the heavens by his name JAH."
- **Jeremiah 33:2:** "...the LORD is his name."
- **Amos 5:8 and 9:6:** "...the LORD is his name."
- **Isaiah 12:2 and 26:4:** "the LORD JEHOVAH" (YAH YHWH in Hebrew).

**Other names God gives or is given**

- **Genesis 17:1:** "I am the Almighty God" (El Shaddai).
- **Exodus 34:14:** "...the LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God."
- **Isaiah 43:11:** "I, even I, am the LORD; and beside me there is no saviour."

**New Testament**

- **John 8:58:** Jesus says, "Before Abraham was, I am," echoing the Exodus 3 phrasing.
- **John 17:6, 26:** Jesus says he has declared the Father's name to his disciples.
- **Revelation 1:8:** "I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending, saith the Lord."

Related passage: **Exodus 20:7** is the commandment not to take the LORD's [[Gate of Wisdom/N/Name\|Name]] in vain.
