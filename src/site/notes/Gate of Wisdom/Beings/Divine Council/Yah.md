"Yah" (יָהּ) is the short form of [[Gate of Wisdom/Beings/Divine Council/YHWH\|YHWH]]. English Bibles usually render it as "LORD," so it is hidden. 

- **Exodus 15:2:** "Yah is my strength and my song." The same line is repeated in Psalm 118:14 and Isaiah 12:2.
- **Exodus 17:16:** "...a hand upon the throne of Yah." The Hebrew text here is disputed.
- **Isaiah 12:2:** "Yah YHWH is my strength and song."
- **Isaiah 26:4:** "...for in Yah YHWH is everlasting strength."
- **Isaiah 38:11:** "I said, I shall not see Yah, even Yah, in the land of the living."
- **Psalm 68:4 (Hebrew 68:5):** "...extol him that rides upon the clouds, by his name Yah."
- **Psalm 68:18 (68:19):** "...that Yah God might dwell there."
- **Psalm 77:11 (77:12):** "I will remember the works of Yah."
- **Psalm 89:8 (89:9):** "O YHWH God of hosts, who is mighty like you, Yah?"
- **Psalm 94:7, 12:** "Yah does not see," and "Blessed is the man whom you chasten, Yah."
- **Psalm 102:18 (102:19):** "...the people yet to be created shall praise Yah."
- **Psalm 115:17:** "The dead do not praise Yah."
- **Psalm 118:5, 14, 17, 18, 19:** The psalmist calls on Yah, calls Yah his strength and song, declares the works of Yah, says Yah has chastened him, and enters to praise Yah.
- **Psalm 122:4:** "...the tribes of Yah."
- **Psalm 130:3:** "If you, Yah, should mark iniquities..."
- **Psalm 135:4:** "For Yah has chosen Jacob for himself."
- **Psalm 150:6:** "Let everything that has breath praise Yah."
- **Song of Solomon 8:6:** "...a flame of Yah" (shalhevetyah). This reading is debated.

**"Hallelu-Yah" ("Praise Yah")**  
This phrase appears about 24 times, all in the Psalms: 104:35; 105:45; 106:1, 48; 111:1; 112:1; 113:1, 9; 115:18; 116:19; 117:2; 135:1, 3, 21; 146:1, 10; 147:1, 20; 148:1, 14; 149:1, 9; 150:1, 6.

**Related**  
Many Hebrew names end in -yah, including Elijah (Eliyahu, "my God is Yah"), Isaiah (Yeshayahu) and Zechariah (Zekaryah). The shortened form in Psalm 68:4 and Exodus 15:2 is the one most often pointed to as YHWH/God's name.

Hebrew verse numbers sometimes run one ahead of English numbering in the Psalms, so both are given where they differ.
