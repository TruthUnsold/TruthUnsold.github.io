# Define

- Hebrew: sheqer (שָׁקֶר) — falsehood, lie, deception, "that which is empty or without basis." 
	- In Exodus 20:16 it modifies ed: ed sheqer (עֵד שָׁקֶר), "witness of falsehood." 
	- Hebrew mindset: sheqer is not only a wrong statement but a statement that leaves a false foundation under someone else's life. 
	- It is the opposite of emet (אֱמֶת), the truth that can be leaned on (Proverbs 12:19; Jeremiah 9:3-5). 
	- It covers 
		- false testimony (Deuteronomy 19:18), 
		- false prophecy (Jeremiah 14:14; 23:25), 
		- false oaths (Leviticus 19:12), 
		- and false worship (Jeremiah 13:25).
- Greek (LXX): pseudes (ψευδής) — false, lying. 
	- John 8:44 and Ephesians 4:25.

# Related Words

- emet (אֱמֶת) — truth, firmness, reliability — Exodus 18:21; Psalm 119:160

- mirmah (מִרְמָה) — deceit, treachery — Psalm 5:6; Proverbs 12:5

- yafiach kezavim (יָפִיחַ כְּזָבִים) — "breathes out lies," the false witness in Proverbs 6:19; 14:5, 25; 19:5, 9
- pseudos (ψεῦδος) — Greek lie — John 8:44; 2 Thessalonians 2:9, 11
- pseustes (ψεύστης) — Greek liar — John 8:44; 1 John 2:4
- pseudoprophetes (ψευδοπροφήτης) — Greek false prophet — Matthew 7:15

# Additional Information

- Sheqer is placed on Yahweh's own list of things He hates: "a lying tongue... a false witness who utters lies, and he who sows discord among brothers" (Proverbs 6:16-19).
- The penalty fits the offense: the false witness is given what he intended for the accused. "You shall do to him as he had thought to do to his brother" (Deuteronomy 19:18-21).
- Proverbs 19:5, 9 says the false witness will not go unpunished and "shall perish."
- The prophets name sheqer as the root of the people's decay: "They have taught their tongue to speak lies" (Jeremiah 9:3-6).
- Sheqer in Leviticus 19:11-12 links stealing, lying, and swearing falsely by Yahweh's name, which ties the eighth and ninth commands to the third (Exodus 20:7, 15-16).
- Truth is the covenant standard: "These are the things that you shall do: speak every man the truth with his neighbor; judge truth and the judgment of peace in your gates" (Zechariah 8:16).
- In the NT the liar's source is the adversary: "he is a liar, and the father of lies" (John 8:44). Revelation places all liars outside the city (Revelation 21:8, 27; 22:15).
