# Define

- Hebrew: ed (עֵד) — witness, one who testifies to what he has seen or heard. 
	- Hebrew mindset: testimony was the mechanism of justice in the gate, since no case was established without witnesses (Deuteronomy 17:6; 19:15). A witness held another person's life, land, and name in his mouth, and the witness also carried the first hand in carrying out the sentence (Deuteronomy 17:7).
- Greek: martyria (μαρτυρία) — testimony, evidence. 
	- The related noun martys (μάρτυς) renders ed in Deuteronomy 19:15, and in the NT it is "witness" (Matthew 18:16; Acts 1:8).

# Additional Information

- One witness is not enough: "at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall a matter be established" (Deuteronomy 19:15; Numbers 35:30).
- Exodus 23:1-3, 6-8 expands the command to the whole court: do not spread a false report, do not follow a crowd to do evil, do not tilt a verdict toward the poor or the great, and do not slay the innocent.
- Leviticus 19:15-16 joins the command to the court and the community: do not pervert justice, and "you shall not go up and down as a slanderer among your people."
- Naboth was killed on the testimony of two "wicked men," set up by Jezebel to testify that he cursed God and the king (1 Kings 21:8-14).
- Yeshua was tried on false testimony, although the witnesses did not agree (Matthew 26:59-61; Mark 14:55-59). Stephen was charged the same way (Acts 6:11-14).
- A righteous witness is the opposite pattern: "A truthful witness delivers souls" (Proverbs 14:25). 
- The ninth command also reaches the one who withholds true testimony: "If anyone sins, in that he hears the voice of adjuration, he being a witness, whether he has seen or known, if he doesn't report it, then he shall bear his iniquity" (Leviticus 5:1).
- Yahweh is Himself called a witness: "Yahweh be witness between me and you" (Genesis 31:50; Malachi 3:5), and Israel is called His witness (Isaiah 43:10, 12).
- Heaven and earth are called witnesses Deuteronomy 30:19: "I call heaven and earth to witness against you today, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse. ..."
