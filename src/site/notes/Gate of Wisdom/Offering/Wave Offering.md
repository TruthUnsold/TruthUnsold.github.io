# Define

- Hebrew: tenufah (תְּנוּפָה) — from the root _nuf_ (נוּף) — to wave, to move back and forth, to brandish; the wave offering is a priestly motion — the offering is held and moved toward the altar and back, a gesture of presenting to Yahweh and receiving back from Him; the movement itself enacts the transaction of dedication
- Greek (LXX): aphorizma (ἀφόρισμα) — or in some LXX texts epirrismos (ἐπιρρισμός) — set-apart portion, that which is separated and dedicated; the Greek loses the motion of the Hebrew tenufah and leans toward the separated/dedicated sense rather than the waving gesture itself — a meaningful distinction showing how the Greek mindset focused on the result of the act rather than the act itself

# Related Words

- _terumah_ (תְּרוּמָה) — [[Gate of Wisdom/Offering/Heave Offering\|Heave Offering]] / lifted contribution; often paired with _tenufah_ in Leviticus and Numbers; where _tenufah_ is waved horizontally, _terumah_ is lifted vertically — together they cover the full gesture of presentation
- _korban_ (קָרְבָּן) — offering, that which is brought near; the broader category under which _tenufah_ falls
- _minchah_ (מִנְחָה) — grain offering; sometimes waved before Yahweh (Lev. 23:11)

# Additional Information

- The wave offering appears across several distinct contexts in Torah:
    - **Exodus 29:24-27** — the breast of the ram of ordination waved before Yahweh at the consecration of Aaron and his sons; the breast becomes the portion of Aaron and his sons _forever_
    - **Leviticus 23:11** — the sheaf of [[Gate of Wisdom/Appointed Time/First Fruits\|First Fruits]] ([[Gate of Wisdom/O/omer\|omer]]) waved before Yahweh on the day after the [[Gate of Wisdom/Appointed Time/Sabbath\|Sabbath]] — the gesture marks the beginning of the count toward [[Gate of Wisdom/Appointed Time/Shavuot\|Shavuot]]
    - **Leviticus 23:20** — two loaves waved at Shavuot with the firstfruits of the wheat harvest
    - **Numbers 6:20** — the Nazirite's offerings waved at the completion of the vow
- The **back-and-forth motion** toward the altar is significant — it is not merely a presentation but a **dialogical gesture**; offered to Yahweh, returned as holy portion to the priest; a picture of gift and reception

# Connections

- Exodus 29:24-27 — priestly ordination wave offering; the breast becomes the priests' perpetual portion
- Leviticus 23:11 — firstfruits sheaf waved; connects to the resurrection timing in NT (1 Cor. 15:20 — Yeshua as _firstfruits_ of those who sleep)
- Leviticus 23:20 — Shavuot wave offering of two loaves
- Numbers 6:20 — Nazirite completion wave offering
- 1 Corinthians 15:20 — _aparche_ (ἀπαρχή) — firstfruits; the wave offering of the _omer_ and Yeshua's resurrection on that same calendar day creates a direct typological connection
