_terumah_ (תְּרוּמָה) — [[Gate of Wisdom/Offering/Heave Offering\|Heave Offering]] / lifted contribution; often paired with [[Gate of Wisdom/Offering/Wave Offering\|Wave Offering]] (_tenufah_) in Leviticus and Numbers; where _tenufah_ is waved horizontally, _terumah_ is lifted vertically — together they cover the full gesture of presentation
