>[!Note]+ Command/Commandment vs Commands/Commandments
>- Love is the one commandment, and the commandments are the many ways it is lived out.
>- In Deuteronomy the Torah speaks of “the commandment” (mitzvah) as a unified whole in Deut 6:25; 8:1; 11:8; 30:11, and of “commandments” (mitzvot) as the individual instructions in Deut 6:2; 8:6; 11:1. The Hebrew mindset sees one instruction made up of many.
>- It matches Jesus's pattern: “On these two commandments hang the whole law and the prophets” (Matt 22:36–40; Deut 6:5; Lev 19:18)

## Audience

- "The chosen lady and her children" (2 John 1:1), 
	- with "the children of your chosen sister" sending greetings (2 John 1:13).
	-  "Chosen" translates eklektē (ἐκλεκτή), "elect, chosen." It is the same idea as Israel as chosen (Deut 7:6; Isa 43:20).
- Whether "lady" is a woman or a congregation is not stated. 
	- Her "children" are said to walk in truth (2 John 1:4), 
	- The household is told how to treat traveling teachers (2 John 1:10), which fits a house gathering.
- Addressed as ones loved by all "who know the truth" (2 John 1:1–2).

## The Truth
- John never gives a one-line definition. 
	- The Word of the Father is truth (John 17:17). 
	- Yeshua is that Word made flesh (John 1:14) and is the truth (John 14:6). 
	- The Spirit is the truth who points to Him (John 16:13–14; 1 John 5:6).
		- A close parallel is John 14:16-17 where Jesus promised his disciples that the Spirit (Paraclete) would be with them forever: “He remains with you and will be in you.”
- In 2 John the truth has three parts: 
	- the person (the Son, 2 John 1:3, 9), 
	- the teaching (2 John 1:9–10), and 
	- the walk (2 John 1:4, 6). 
		- John’s own words in 2 John 1:6 tie the walk to the commandments: “This is love, that we should walk according to his commandments.”

Sources:

- 2 John 1:1–13
- 1 John 2:7, 18, 22; 4:3; 3 John 1:1, 3–8
- Deuteronomy 7:6; Isaiah 43:20

## The Teachings of Christ

John never gives a labeled list called "the teachings of Christ," but he states what the teaching contains in 2 John 1:5–9 and spells out the same points across his writings. They fall into three groups: 
- who Yeshua is, 
- how His people live, 
- and remaining in Him.

Term: Teaching
- **Greek**: didachē (διδαχή), "teaching, instruction."
- **Hebrew equivalent**: [[Gate of Wisdom/T/Torah\|Torah]] (תּוֹרָה), "instruction, direction," and lekach (לֶקַח), "teaching, learning" (Prov 4:2; Deut 32:2). In the Hebrew mindset a teaching is something received and walked in, not only believed.

### Who Yeshua is

- **He came in the flesh**: 2 John 1:7; 1 John 4:2–3; John 1:14
- **He is the Messiah, the Son of God**: 1 John 2:22; 5:1, 5; John 20:31
- **The Father and the Son are not separated**: 1 John 2:23 ("Whoever denies the Son, the same doesn't have the Father"); 2 John 1:9
- **Eternal life is in the Son**: 1 John 5:11–12; John 17:3
- **The testimony of water, blood, and Spirit**: 1 John 5:6–8

### How His people live

- **Love one another**: 2 John 1:5; 1 John 3:11, 23; John 13:34; 15:12
- **Keep His commandments**: John 14:15, 21; 1 John 2:3–4; 5:3; 2 John 1:6
- **Walk as He walked**: 1 John 2:6
- **Love in deed and truth**: 1 John 3:16–18
- **Confess sins; sin is lawlessness**: 1 John 1:9; 2:1–2; 3:4
- **Believe in His name**: 1 John 3:23

### Remaining in Him

- **Remain in His word**: John 8:31–32
- **Remain in Him**: John 15:4–10
- **Remain in what was heard from the beginning**: 1 John 2:24–25. This reads closely with 2 John 1:9. If it remains, "you also will remain in the Son and in the Father."
- **The anointing teaches and remains**: 1 John 2:27

### Observations from Scripture

- **John says the teaching is the Father's, given through the Son.** John 7:16–17 and 14:24 ("the word which you hear isn't mine, but the Father's who sent me"). So "the teaching of Christ" in 2 John 1:9 leads to having "both the Father and the Son."
- **His teaching.** In John 18:19 the high priest asks Yeshua about "his teaching" (didachē, διδαχή), and this is the longest record John gives of it.
