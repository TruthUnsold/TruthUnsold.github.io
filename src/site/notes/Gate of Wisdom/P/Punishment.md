Word: Punishment — Genesis 4:13; Leviticus 26:18, 41; Exodus 34:7; Proverbs 3:11-12; Matthew 25:46; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 12:6

# Define

- Hebrew: avon (עָוֹן) — iniquity, the guilt of it, and the punishment it brings, all in one word. Cain says "My punishment is greater than I can bear" (Genesis 4:13, WEB). Hebrew mindset: the wrong act and its consequence are one thing. Sin is carried like a weight (Leviticus 5:1; Isaiah 53:6).
- Hebrew: paqad (פָּקַד) — to visit or attend to, including visiting iniquity for judgment (Exodus 34:7; Exodus 20:5).
- Hebrew: yasar (יָסַר) / musar (מוּסָר) — to discipline or chastise, correction aimed at return. Leviticus 26:18, 28 (chastised sevenfold); Proverbs 3:11-12; Isaiah 53:5.
- Hebrew: naqam (נָקַם) / nekamah (נְקָמָה) — vengeance, reserved to YHWH: Deuteronomy 32:35, 41.
- Hebrew: ratsah avon (רָצָה עָוֹן) — to "accept" or pay off the punishment of iniquity, Leviticus 26:41.
- Greek (LXX): paideuo (παιδεύω) / paideia (παιδεία) — LXX for yasar and musar (Proverbs 3:11-12; Leviticus 26:18).
- Greek (LXX): ekdikesis (ἐκδίκησις) — LXX for naqam (Deuteronomy 32:35).
- Greek: kolasis (κόλασις) — punishment, chastisement: "eternal punishment" (Matthew 25:46, WEB); also 1 John 4:18.
- Greek: timoria (τιμωρία) — punishment as retribution: "how much worse punishment" (Hebrews 10:29, WEB).
- Greek: paideia (παιδεία) — discipline of a son by his father (Hebrews 12:5-11).
- Greek: ekdikesis (ἐκδίκησις) — vengeance (Romans 12:19; 2 Thessalonians 1:8).

# Related Words

- Curse: qelalah (קְלָלָה) — Deuteronomy 28:15; Greek katara (κατάρα) — Galatians 3:10
- Wrath: chemah (חֵמָה) / aph (אַף); Greek orge (ὀργή) — Romans 2:5, 8
- Judgment: mishpat (מִשְׁפָּט); Greek krima (κρίμα) — Ecclesiastes 12:14; Revelation 20:12-13
- Reward or recompense: gemul (גְּמוּל) — Isaiah 3:11; Greek apodidomi (ἀποδίδωμι) — Romans 2:6

# Additional Information

- Punishment is graded: those who know and reject receive more (Hebrews 10:28-29).
- It can be corrective: "whom YHWH loves he corrects" (Proverbs 3:12; Hebrews 12:6).
- It can be final: Matthew 25:46; 2 Thessalonians 1:9.
- Consequence is sown and reaped: Galatians 6:7-8; Hosea 8:7.
- The Judge is merciful: Exodus 34:6-7; Psalm 103:10; Ezekiel 18:23, 32.
- YHWH takes on the punishment Himself: Isaiah 53:5-6; 1 Peter 2:24.
