# Define

- Hebrew: ma'aser (מַעֲשֵׂר) — a tenth part; the concept carries the sense of setting apart one part in ten as belonging to Yahweh from the whole; not a tax but a recognition of Yahweh's ownership of the entire yield; the tithe acknowledges that the harvest belongs to Him before it belongs to the one who worked it
- Greek (LXX): apodekatoo (ἀποδεκατόω) — to tithe, to take or give a tenth; the compound _apo_ (from) + _dekatos_ (tenth) — the Greek carries the transactional sense of the tenth being removed from the whole and given over

# Related Words

- _bikkurim_ (בִּכּוּרִים) — [[Gate of Wisdom/Appointed Time/First Fruits\|First Fruits]]; related in concept — the first and the tenth both mark Yahweh's prior claim on the whole
- _qodesh_ (קֹדֶשׁ) —[[Gate of Wisdom/H/Holy\|Holy]] holiness, set-apartness; the tithe is set apart in the same sense — removed from common use and dedicated to Yahweh
- _terumah_ (תְּרוּמָה) — heave offering / contribution; appears alongside _ma'aser_ in Deuteronomy 12:6 as a distinct but related category of dedicated portion

# Additional Information

- Three distinct tithe laws appear in Torah:
    - **Deuteronomy 14:22-27** — the tithe brought to the chosen place and eaten there in rejoicing before Yahweh; if the journey is too far, convert to silver and purchase what you desire at the place
    - **Deuteronomy 14:28-29** — the third-year tithe stored in the gates for the Levite, stranger, orphan, and widow
    - **Numbers 18:21-24** — the tithe given to the Levites as their inheritance in place of land
- Malachi 3:10 — withholding the tithe is framed as robbing Yahweh (_qava'_ — קָבַע); bringing the whole tithe to the storehouse is tied to an explicit promise of opened windows of heaven

# Connections

- Deuteronomy 14:22-29 — full elaboration of the tithe law
- Numbers 18:21-24 — Levitical tithe
- Malachi 3:10 — robbing Yahweh; blessing tied to full tithe
- Genesis 14:20 — Abraham gives a tenth to Melchizedek — first tithe in Scripture, predating Sinai
- Genesis 28:22 — Jacob vows a tenth to Yahweh at Bethel
