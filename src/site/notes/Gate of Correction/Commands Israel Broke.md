# Commands Israel Broke

## Summary List

- **No other gods before Yahweh** — "You shall have no other gods before me." — Exodus 20:3; Deuteronomy 5:7
- **No carved images or idols** — "You shall not make for yourself a carved image, or any likeness of anything that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth. You shall not bow yourself down to them, nor serve them." — Exodus 20:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:8–9
- **Do not take the Name of Yahweh in vain** — "You shall not take the name of Yahweh your God in vain." — Exodus 20:7; Deuteronomy 5:11
- **Remember the Sabbath and keep it holy** — "Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy." — Exodus 20:8; Deuteronomy 5:12
- **Honor your father and mother** — "Honor your father and your mother." — Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16
- **Do not murder** — "You shall not murder." — Exodus 20:13; Deuteronomy 5:17
- **Do not commit adultery** — "You shall not commit adultery." — Exodus 20:14; Deuteronomy 5:18
- **Do not steal** — "You shall not steal." — Exodus 20:15; Deuteronomy 5:19
- **Do not bear false witness** — "You shall not give false testimony against your neighbor." — Exodus 20:16; Deuteronomy 5:20
- **Do not covet** — "You shall not covet your neighbor's house. You shall not covet your neighbor's wife." — Exodus 20:17; Deuteronomy 5:21
- **Do not make molten gods** — "You shall make no molten gods for yourselves." — Exodus 34:17; Leviticus 19:4
- **Do not oppress the foreigner** — "You shall not oppress a foreigner." — Exodus 22:21; 23:9; Leviticus 19:33
- **Do not afflict widows or fatherless children** — "You shall not afflict any widow or fatherless child." — Exodus 22:22
- **Do not charge interest to the poor** — "You shall not be to him as a creditor; neither shall you charge him interest." — Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:37
- **Do not follow other gods or serve them** — "You shall not bow down to their gods, nor serve them, nor follow their practices." — Exodus 23:24; Deuteronomy 6:14
- **Do not make a covenant with the inhabitants of Canaan** — "You shall make no covenant with them, nor with their gods." — Exodus 23:32; Deuteronomy 7:2
- **Do not let them dwell in the land** — "They shall not dwell in your land." — Exodus 23:33
- **Keep the Sabbath — it is a sign forever** — "Surely you shall keep my Sabbaths, for it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations." — Exodus 31:13; Ezekiel 20:12
- **Do not sacrifice to any god but Yahweh** — "He who sacrifices to any god, except to Yahweh only, shall be utterly destroyed." — Exodus 22:20
- **Do not deny justice to the poor** — "You shall not deny justice to your poor people in their lawsuits." — Exodus 23:6; Leviticus 19:15
- **Do not spread a false report** — "You shall not spread a false report." — Exodus 23:1
- **Do not take a bribe** — "You shall take no bribe." — Exodus 23:8; Deuteronomy 16:19
- **Let the land rest in the seventh year** — "The seventh year you shall let it rest and lie fallow." — Exodus 23:10–11; Leviticus 25:4
- **Bring firstfruits to the house of Yahweh** — "You shall bring the first of the first fruits of your ground into the house of Yahweh your God." — Exodus 23:19; 34:26
- **Do not invoke the names of other gods** — "Don't invoke the name of other gods, nor let them be heard out of your mouth." — Exodus 23:13
- **Do not do as the nations do** — "You shall not walk in the customs of the nation which I am casting out before you." — Leviticus 20:23
- **Do not shed innocent blood** — "Do not shed innocent blood in this place." — Deuteronomy 19:10; Jeremiah 7:6
- **Do not intermarry with the nations** — "You shall not make marriages with them." — Deuteronomy 7:3
- **Destroy idolatrous places of worship** — "You shall surely destroy all the places in which the nations served their gods." — Deuteronomy 12:2–3
- **Love Yahweh with all your heart** — "You shall love Yahweh your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your might." — Deuteronomy 6:5
- **Do not add to or take away from the commands** — "You shall not add to the word which I command you, neither shall you take away from it." — Deuteronomy 4:2; 12:32
- **Do not practice divination or sorcery** — "There shall not be found with you anyone who practices divination, or tells fortunes, or an enchanter, or a sorcerer." — Deuteronomy 18:10–11
- **Do not go after Baal** — implicit in the first and second commandments; explicit charge — "They followed Baal." — Judges 2:13; 1 Kings 16:31
- **Honor Yahweh with firstfruits** — "Honor Yahweh with your substance, with the first fruits of all your increase." — Proverbs 3:9

# The Weight of Israel's Rebellion

- Yahweh's anger was kindled against Israel and He sold them into the hands of their enemies — Plunder, oppression, and military defeat were the direct consequences of covenant breaking
- The nation was handed over to foreign powers in cycles — each period of oppression in the book of Judges directly tied to specific acts of covenant unfaithfulness
- The northern kingdom of Israel was carried into Assyrian exile and did not return — the land was repopulated with foreign peoples; ten tribes effectively lost to covenant community
- The southern kingdom of Judah was carried into Babylonian exile for seventy years — corresponding precisely to the number of Sabbath years the land had not rested
- The land itself vomited out its inhabitants — the land's exile was Yahweh keeping His own Sabbaths that Israel had denied it
- The temple was destroyed — first by Nebuchadnezzar in 586 BC; the very house of Yahweh was burned because those who served in it had profaned it
- The ark of the covenant was captured by the Philistines — Israel's central covenant object removed from them as a consequence of presumptuous battle without Yahweh's blessing
- Famine, drought, and agricultural failure were sent — Yahweh closed the heavens over a land that withheld firstfruits, robbed tithes, and broke Sabbath years
- Sword, pestilence, and plague came upon the people — the covenant curses of Deuteronomy 28 executed in full sequence
- The priesthood became corrupt and was judged — Eli's house cut off; the sons of Eli killed on the same day; later, priests who offered polluted sacrifices shamed before all the people
- Kings who turned to other gods saw their dynasties cut off — Saul's kingdom torn from him; Solomon's kingdom divided; Jeroboam's line annihilated
- Child sacrifice to Molech and demons required blood atonement that no individual offering could cover — this was a high-handed sin bringing national judgment
- No single sin offering covered the nation's intentional, repeated, high-handed covenant violations — only national repentance, return, and Yahweh's own mercy could restore what was broken
- Yahweh's mercy remained open — even in exile, return and restoration were promised upon genuine repentance and turning back to the covenant with the whole heart
