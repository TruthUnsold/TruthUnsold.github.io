# 2 John

[[_The Scrolls/4. The Gospels and The Apostles/63 - 2 John/2 John-01\|Start Reading →]]

## Author and Date

- **Traditional author**: John the apostle. The letter itself names the writer only as "the elder" (2 John 1:1), as does 3 John 1:1.
- **Dating**: Not stated in the text. Traditionally placed in the late first century (c. AD 85–95), alongside 1 John, which shares its vocabulary: "from the beginning" (2 John 1:5; 1 John 2:7), "walking in truth" (2 John 1:4; 3 John 1:3–4), "antichrist" (2 John 1:7; 1 John 2:18, 22; 4:3).
- **Writer's title**: presbyteros (πρεσβύτερος), "elder."

## Audience

- "The chosen lady and her children" (2 John 1:1), 
	- with "the children of your chosen sister" sending greetings (2 John 1:13).
	-  "Chosen" translates eklektē (ἐκλεκτή), "elect, chosen." It is the same idea as Israel as chosen (Deut 7:6; Isa 43:20).
- Whether "lady" is a woman or a congregation is not stated. 
	- Her "children" are said to walk in truth (2 John 1:4), 
	- The household is told how to treat traveling teachers (2 John 1:10), which fits a house gathering.
- Addressed as ones loved by all "who know the truth" (2 John 1:1–2).

## Setting and Main Storyline

- **Setting:**
    - Itinerant teachers are going out into the world (2 John 1:7).
    - Some deny that Yeshua the Messiah came in the flesh (2 John 1:7).
    - Households hosted traveling teachers (2 John 1:10; compare 3 John 1:5–8).
    - The writer prefers a face-to-face visit over paper and ink (2 John 1:12).
- **Main Storyline:**
    - The elder greets the household with grace, mercy, and peace from the Father and the Son, in truth and love (2 John 1:1–3).
    - He rejoices that some of her children walk in truth (2 John 1:4).
    - He urges the old commandment to love one another, and defines love as walking according to His commandments (2 John 1:5–6).
    - He warns of deceivers who deny the Messiah came in the flesh (2 John 1:7).
    - He tells them to watch themselves so they do not lose what has been worked for (2 John 1:8).
    - He says that whoever goes beyond and does not remain in the teaching of the Messiah does not have God, and whoever remains has both the Father and the Son (2 John 1:9).
    - He forbids receiving or greeting anyone who does not bring this teaching, because greeting such a one shares in his evil works (2 John 1:10–11).
    - He closes by hoping to come in person, with greetings from the chosen sister's children (2 John 1:12–13).

## Where and When

- **Places named in the text**: None. The writer's location and the recipients' location are not stated.
- **Traditional location (outside the text)**: Ephesus and the churches of Asia, in modern western Turkey near Selçuk. This comes from church tradition, not from the letter.
- **Time period**: Late first century AD, after the Messiah's death and resurrection and within the apostolic generation. The phrase "from the beginning" (2 John 1:5, 6) points back to the original teaching.

## Characters and People Groups

- **The elder**: the writer (2 John 1:1)
- **The chosen lady**: the recipient (2 John 1:1, 5)
- **Her children**: some walking in truth (2 John 1:1, 4)
- **The chosen sister and her children**: send greetings (2 John 1:13)
- **God the Father** (2 John 1:3, 4, 9)
- **Yeshua the Messiah, the Son of the Father** (2 John 1:3, 7, 9)
- **Those who know the truth** (2 John 1:1)
- **The deceivers / the antichrist**: planos (πλάνος), "deceiver," and antichristos (ἀντίχριστος), "against or in place of the Messiah" (2 John 1:7)
- **Traveling teachers**: those who come bringing, or not bringing, "this teaching" (2 John 1:10)

## Symbolic Roles

- **Writer / Shepherd-Overseer**: The elder (2 John 1:1)
- **Chosen / Beloved**: The lady and her children (2 John 1:1)
- **Truth-Walkers**: The children walking in truth (2 John 1:4)
- **Source of grace, mercy, and peace**: God the Father and Yeshua the Son (2 John 1:3)
- **Deceiver**: Those who deny the Messiah came in the flesh (2 John 1:7)
- **Gatekeeper**: The household that must not receive those who do not bring the teaching (2 John 1:10)
- **Partaker in evil works**: Anyone who greets the deceiver (2 John 1:11)
