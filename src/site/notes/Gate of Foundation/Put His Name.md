# Where Yahweh Has Placed His Name


The Hebrew behind put his name is _lasim et shemo_ (לָשִׂים אֶת־שְׁמוֹ) — to set, to place, to establish His [[Gate of Wisdom/N/Name\|Name]] as a permanent fixture at a location. This is not merely association but active divine designation.

### Shiloh - Destroyed

- The tabernacle's first fixed location after the conquest (Josh. 18:1)
- Yahweh's name dwelt there during the period of the judges
- _"the place in Shiloh where I caused my name to dwell at the first"_ — #24Jer/Jeremiah07 :12
- Destroyed — Psalm 78:60 — _"He forsook the tabernacle of Shiloh, the tent he placed among men"_

### Jerusalem — Mount Moriah - Name Departed

- David brings the ark to Jerusalem (2 Sam. 6)
- Yahweh refuses David the temple but chooses the site (2 Sam. 7:13)
- Solomon builds and Yahweh confirms:
    - _"I have chosen Jerusalem that my name might be there"_ — #142Chron/2Chronicles06:6
    - _"I have hallowed this house which you have built to put my name there forever"_ — 1 Kings 9:3
- The chosen place of Deuteronomy 12 finds its fulfillment here
- The name departs with the glory (_kavod_ — כָּבוֹד) in #26Ezek/Ezekiel10 -11 — Ezekiel watches the glory leave the temple, hover over the Mount of Olives, and withdraw eastward

### The Second Temple - Name Departed

- Built after the return from exile (Ezra 6)
- Notably the rabbinical tradition acknowledges five things missing from the second temple that were present in the first — including the _Shekinah_ glory
- Yahweh's name is associated with the location but the full manifestation of His presence is absent
- Yeshua enters this temple and declares it _"my Father's house"_ (John 2:16) — and later _"your house is left to you desolate"_ (Matt. 23:38) — the name departing again

### In Yeshua

- John 1:14 — _"the Word became flesh and tabernacled (_eskenosen_ — ἐσκήνωσεν) among us"_
- The name Yahweh saves — _Yeshua_ (יֵשׁוּעַ) — is itself the divine name in human form
- Philippians 2:9 — _"God highly exalted him and gave him the name which is above every name"_
- The place of the name shifts from a geographic location to a person

### In the Assembly - Current

- 1 Corinthians 3:16 — _"you are a temple of God and the Spirit of God dwells in you"_
- Ephesians 2:21-22 — _"the whole building fitted together grows into a holy temple in the Lord... a dwelling of God in the Spirit"_
- The name is placed among a people rather than a location during this age

### The Restored Jerusalem - Future

- Ezekiel 40-48 describes a rebuilt temple in extraordinary detail — proportions, gates, courts, chambers, the prince's portion, redistributed tribal land
- The glory returns:
    - _"The glory of Yahweh came into the house by the way of the gate whose front is toward the east"_ — #26Ezek/Ezekiel43:4
    - _"This is the place of my throne and the place of the soles of my feet where I will dwell among the children of Israel forever"_ — Ezekiel 43:7
- The city receives a new name at the close of Ezekiel:
    - _"The name of the city from that day shall be Yahweh Is There"_ — _Yahweh Shammah_ (יְהוָה שָׁמָּה) — Ezekiel 48:35
    - The name of the city becomes the name of Yahweh's presence — geography and divine name fully merged

### Zechariah's Witness - Future

- _"Many nations shall join themselves to Yahweh in that day and shall be my people and I will dwell in the midst of you"_ — Zechariah 2:11
- _"Yahweh will be king over all the earth. In that day Yahweh will be one and his name one"_ — Zechariah 14:9
- The name placed at one location expands to universal singularity — one name over all the earth

### The Nations Come to the Name - Future

- Isaiah 2:2-3 — all nations flow to the mountain of Yahweh's house; Torah goes forth from Zion
- Micah 4:1-2 — the same vision; the place of the name becomes the destination of the nations
- Zechariah 14:16 — the nations go up year by year to worship the King and keep Sukkot — the feast of Yahweh's dwelling (_sukkah_ — סֻכָּה)
### The New Jerusalem - Future

- Revelation 21:2-3 — _"I saw the holy city, New Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God... Behold the tabernacle of God is with men and he will dwell with them"_
- The word tabernacle here is _skene_ (σκηνή) — the Greek equivalent of the Hebrew _mishkan_ (מִשְׁכָּן) — the dwelling place; the entire trajectory of the name dwelling in a place reaches its fullness
- Revelation 21:22 — _"I saw no temple in it for the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple"_
    - The place disappears — Yahweh and the Lamb are the temple; the name is no longer placed in a location because the location is the name
- Revelation 21:3 — _"They will be his peoples and God himself will be with them and be their God"_
- Revelation 22:4 — _"They shall see his face and his name shall be on their foreheads"_
    - The name moves from geography (Shiloh, Jerusalem) to person (Yeshua) to community (the assembly) to written on the people themselves — the final placement of the name

The arc of Scripture is the arc of the name finding its dwelling — moving from tent to stone to flesh to people to the whole of the new creation.
