One-Year Torah Portion Plan — Oct 25, 2026 to Oct 23, 2027

  • 52 weeks, Sunday (Day 1) through Saturday/Shabbat (Day 7)
    • Weeks with festival (Appointed Time) readings (Passover, Shavuot, Yom HaKippurim, Sukkot, follow the festival readings instead.
  • Torah divided into seven daily readings; Haftarah on Day 6 (Fri); Gospel on Day 7 (Sat)
    • Torah and Haftarah: standard annual cycle.
    • Gospel - Chayyei Yeshua cycle (Year B) for the remaining weeks from She Opens Her Bible
  • Verse numbering follows the WEB (English) versification
